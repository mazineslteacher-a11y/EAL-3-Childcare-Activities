5. Agree or Disagree?

Speaking • 10 minutes
← All activities

Take turns. Give your opinion, a reason and an example. Your partner should respond respectfully.

1. Childcare should be free for all families in Australia.
   
2. Early childhood educators should receive higher wages.
   
3. Parents should pay the full cost of childcare.
   
4. The government should build more childcare centres.
   
5. Childcare is just babysitting, not education.
   

Useful language

In my opinion … • I agree because … • I see your point, but … • On the other hand … • For example …
