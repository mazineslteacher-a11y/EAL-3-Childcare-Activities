1. Think–Pair–Share

Speaking • 5–8 minutes
← All activities

Work with a partner

Student A asks questions 1–3. Student B asks 4–5. Give reasons and examples.

  1. What is childcare?
  2. Why do parents need childcare?
  3. What problems might families experience?
  4. What makes a childcare centre good?
  5. Should the government help families pay for childcare? Why?

Record your ideas

Share one idea with another pair.
